# Sky Stacker ☁️🏙️

A physics-based stacking game where players drop buildings to build the highest tower possible into the sky, featuring dynamic day-and-night cycles, procedural backgrounds, and score tracking.

## Features
- **Physics-Based Mechanics:** Stack blocks carefully while managing balance and the center of mass.
- **Dynamic Environment:** Enjoy a shifting background with smooth day-to-night transitions, moving clouds, and a scenic mountain landscape.
- **Score Tracking:** Keeps track of your current height and saves your all-time **Top Height** locally.
- **Responsive Controls:** Smooth touch and mouse controls for precise block placement.

## Built With
- **HTML5 Canvas** (Game Rendering)
- **JavaScript (ES6+)** (Game Logic & Physics)
- **CSS3** (UI Styling & Glassmorphism)

## How to Play
1. Click or tap to release the falling block.
2. Align it with the tower below to keep it stable.
3. Don't let the tower lean too much or fall into the void, or it's game over!
